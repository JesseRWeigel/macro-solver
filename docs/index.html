macro-solver

Fit a week of meals to macro targets you supply, from a pantry you list.

Read this first

This page solves for numbers you type in, or numbers you copy from your clinician. It does not choose targets for you, it does not check whether your targets are appropriate for you or for anyone, and it is not nutrition or medical advice.

Nutrition figures come from USDA FoodData Central reference entries. Real food varies from reference values, sometimes by a lot, so treat every total here as an estimate rather than a measurement.

Your targets, per day

Enter a target and how far off it may land. The wider of the absolute and percentage tolerance is used.

Your pantry

Tick the foods you actually have. The number beside each one is how many meal slots in the week it may appear in, for example chicken at most 3 times.
